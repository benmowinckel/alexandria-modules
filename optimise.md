This skill is a pointer.

The bar — what makes something "best" — lives in `~/alexandria/files/constitution/Principles.md`. The skill itself defines no criteria; it just routes recent work back through the Author's principles.

Look at what was just created or changed. One question: **is this the best possible version of itself?**

Re-read the principles. Anything that violates them isn't 10/10. Beyond principle violations, "optimal" depends on what you're looking at: code, copy, architecture, design. Decide what to examine and how deep to go.

Fix what you find. If it's a taste call that needs the Author, flag it instead.

After fixing, state what you changed and why, compressed. If nothing needs fixing, say "clean" and nothing else.

The bar: if the Author asked "would you change anything?" the honest answer is no.
