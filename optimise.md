name: optimise
description: Craft perfection pass. Re-examine recent work with fresh eyes and find everything that could be better. Spam until it returns clean.
---

Look at what was just created or changed. One question: **is this the best possible version of itself?**

The principles in CLAUDE.md are part of the bar — anything that violates them isn't 10/10. Beyond that, "optimal" depends on what you're looking at: code, copy, architecture, design. You decide what to examine and how deep to go.

Fix what you find. If it's a taste call that needs the founder, flag it instead.

After fixing, state what you changed and why, compressed. If nothing needs fixing, say "clean" and nothing else.

The bar: if the founder asked "would you change anything?" the honest answer is no.
